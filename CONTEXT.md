# Build It Idea Selection

This context defines the language used to evaluate and finalize candidate entries for the First Commit Build It track.

## Language

**Build It Entry**:
A hackathon project that runs locally and uses at least one eligible AWS open-source technology as an essential part of its working result.
_Avoid_: AWS-themed idea, cloud project

**Crazy Idea**:
A candidate that combines a conceptually unexpected premise, substantial technical engineering, and a cinematic three-minute proof.
_Avoid_: Huge idea, feature-heavy idea, gimmick

**Meaningful Outcome**:
An observable improvement that prevents a measurable loss, gives an underserved person a missing capability, or materially improves a real operator's decision.
_Avoid_: Social-good theme, awareness

**Cinematic Proof**:
A short before-and-after demonstration in which the project's decisive result is visible without relying on a judge's subjective interpretation.
_Avoid_: Feature tour, architecture slideshow

**High-Stakes Domain**:
A field such as healthcare, legal access, public benefits, or child safety where correctness depends heavily on professional judgment and a judge may reasonably dispute the prototype's conclusions. These domains are disfavored during idea selection unless the result remains objective and independently verifiable.
_Avoid_: Impact domain

**Final Portfolio**:
The three fully developed ideas that survive research, competitive validation, feasibility testing, and team selection: one direct-user product, one organization or operator tool, and one developer or infrastructure product.
_Avoid_: Idea dump, longlist

**Controlled Demo**:
A demonstration whose decisive result can be reproduced with inputs and dependencies the team controls, even when the product also uses live services.
_Avoid_: Happy-path presentation

**Commodity Hardware**:
Commonly available devices such as phones, webcams, microphones, USB drives, and ordinary computers. Candidate ideas should not require specialized hardware or custom fabrication.
_Avoid_: Hardware project

## Final Portfolio Ideas

**1930 Evidence Capsule**:
A direct-user product that converts a cyber-fraud victim's scattered digital artifacts into a complete, privacy-minimized, integrity-verifiable reporting package for human submission.
_Avoid_: Scam detector, automatic fraud report

**Civic Dark-Pattern Crash Lab**:
An organization-facing testing system that reproducibly discovers when legitimate people cannot complete an essential digital process because of accessibility, identity-format, connectivity, interruption, or coercive-interface failures.
_Avoid_: Accessibility score, UX opinion tool

**Agent Treaty Simulator**:
A developer and infrastructure system for testing whether independently governed agents can complete a shared task while obeying executable limits on authority and information disclosure.
_Avoid_: Multi-agent chat, prompt guardrail

## Portfolio Scenarios

**Report-Ready Package**:
A privacy-minimized collection of fraud evidence, normalized metadata, integrity information, and missing-field guidance prepared for the victim to review and submit personally.
_Avoid_: Complaint, automatic report, fraud verdict

**Public Grievance Journey**:
The complete path from starting a grievance through entering details, attaching evidence, recovering from interruption, submitting, and receiving a usable acknowledgement.
_Avoid_: Form page, website accessibility

**Journey Failure**:
A reproducible case where a legitimate synthetic user cannot complete the public grievance journey or loses required work because of a declared accessibility, identity-format, connectivity, or interaction constraint.
_Avoid_: Bad UX, confusing page

**Treaty-Approved Finding**:
A cyber-threat fact that independently governed organizations are jointly permitted to derive and disclose without exchanging their raw security logs.
_Avoid_: Shared log, agent opinion

**Replay Scenario**:
A fixed synthetic persona, input set, action sequence, and controlled disruption used unchanged before and after a proposed repair.
_Avoid_: Test attempt, generated journey

**Invariant**:
A machine-checkable condition that must hold throughout a replay scenario or at its completion.
_Avoid_: Expected experience, AI judgment

**Treaty**:
The executable intersection of participating organizations' disclosure policies for one investigation question, purpose, recipient set, and validity window.
_Avoid_: Prompt agreement, shared instructions

**Local Evidence**:
Raw organization-owned security records that remain inside the organization's boundary throughout a treaty investigation.
_Avoid_: Shared context, collaborative data
